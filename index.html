From the ground to the report

Do chão ao relatório

My part in a team simulator, in five stages, with who built what.

Minha parte num simulador feito em equipe, em cinco etapas, com quem fez o quê.

ContextContexto

RealDrive Agro is a training simulator for operating agricultural machines: planting, spraying and harvesting, with an evaluation report at the end of each lesson. It was built as a team at RealDrive within AGRO 4.0, a program funded by Finep (Brazil's federal innovation funding agency), and shown at the Brazilian agricultural trade fairs Tecnoshow and Show Rural.

O RealDrive Agro é um simulador de treinamento para operação de máquinas agrícolas: plantio, pulverização e colheita, com relatório de avaliação ao final de cada aula. Foi desenvolvido em equipe na RealDrive dentro do projeto AGRO 4.0, com apoio da Finep, e apresentado em feiras como Tecnoshow e Show Rural.

ResultsResultados
  • Built: the two farms, the crop system (three crops with growth stages, cut and crushed states), the menu flow and garage, and later the lesson telemetry and the PDF report
  • Problem solved: thousands of plants on screen with the simulator still usable, and a lesson that ends in a report the instructor can read
  • Impact: part of a Finep-funded training simulator, shown at the Brazilian agricultural trade fairs Tecnoshow and Show Rural
  • O que construí: as duas fazendas, o sistema de culturas (três culturas com estágios de crescimento, versões cortada e amassada), o fluxo de menus e a garagem, e depois a telemetria das aulas e o relatório em PDF
  • Problema resolvido: milhares de plantas na tela sem travar o simulador, e uma aula que termina num relatório que o instrutor consegue ler
  • Impacto: parte de um simulador de treinamento financiado pela Finep, apresentado na Tecnoshow e no Show Rural
1 · The ground1 · O chão

Before simulating a machine, it needed a place where it made sense. I owned the environment and level design of the two farms, Farm 1 and Farm 2:

  • Terrain, plots and operating areas, aligned to the world origin, plus roads, fences, street lighting, forests, mountains and skyboxes
  • Soybean, corn and wheat with growth stages, in rows (75 cm spacing), with density tuned per operation
  • Cut versions of each crop after harvest, and crushed vegetation behind the machine (GrassBendController)
  • Performance for thousands of plants: LODs, 2D billboards, static crops in the background and render layers
  • Crop loading and spawning through RDSceneManager and the terrain detail layers

Antes de simular uma máquina, era preciso um lugar onde ela fizesse sentido. Fui responsável pelo ambiente e pelo level design das duas fazendas, Farm 1 e Farm 2:

  • Terreno, talhões e áreas operacionais, alinhados à origem do mundo, mais estradas, cercas, iluminação viária, florestas, montanhas e skyboxes
  • Soja, milho e trigo com estágios de crescimento, em linhas (espaçamento de 75 cm), com densidade ajustada para cada operação
  • Versões cortadas de cada cultura depois da colheita, e vegetação amassada atrás da máquina (GrassBendController)
  • Performance para milhares de plantas: LODs, billboards 2D, culturas estáticas no fundo e layers de renderização
  • Carregamento e spawn das culturas pelo RDSceneManager e pelas camadas de detalhe do terreno

  • Scene loading with per-farm loading images, and layouts adapted to different resolutions
  • The garage in the main menu, with colour options (see the Garagem case)
  • First Rewired integration in the farm scenes, and fixes such as the minimap marker disappearing on Farm 1

O caminho entre abrir o simulador e dirigir a máquina, construído com Doozy UI:

3 · Harvest3 · Colheita

A harvest has stages: cut, store, transfer, unload. I created the first version of the harvest controller (RDHarvestClassController: unloading and the grain pile). Vinicius built the grain tank, grain loss and falling grain. I then integrated triggers, unloading points, farm limits and the link to telemetry and the report.

Uma colheita tem etapas: cortar, armazenar, transbordar, descarregar. Criei a primeira versão do controlador de colheita (RDHarvestClassController: descarga e monte de grãos). O Vinicius fez o graneleiro, as perdas e a queda dos grãos. Depois eu integrei gatilhos, pontos de descarga, limites da fazenda e a ligação com a telemetria e o relatório.

4 · Rules, not buttons4 · Regras, não botões

A working machine is not, on its own, training. The flow is: machine state → condition → allowed/blocked action → sequence → detection → feedback → progression. Three people built it: Guilherme Schaeffer the structure, menus and completion UI; Vinicius routes, damage and colliders; I built the procedure sequences and the safety locks.

Uma máquina funcionando não é, sozinha, um treinamento. O fluxo é: estado da máquina → condição → ação permitida/bloqueada → sequência → detecção → feedback → progressão. Três pessoas construíram: o Guilherme Schaeffer a estrutura, os menus e a UI de conclusão; o Vinicius rota, dano e colisores; eu as sequências de procedimento e as travas de segurança.

5 · From simulating to measuring5 · De simular a medir

In 2026 I created the lesson data collection: speed, RPM, throttle, brake, steering, load, position and machine states, about 10 times per second. I created LessonPdfExporter and developed it with Guilherme: at the end of the lesson it generates a PDF with the path drawn as vectors, charts, statistics, grain loss and speed recommendations, opened inside the simulator.

Em 2026 criei a coleta de dados da aula: velocidade, RPM, acelerador, freio, direção, carga, posição e estados da máquina, cerca de 10 vezes por segundo. Criei o LessonPdfExporter e o desenvolvi com o Guilherme: no fim da aula ele gera um PDF com a trajetória desenhada em vetor, gráficos, estatísticas, perda de grãos e recomendações de velocidade, aberto dentro do simulador.

From the real orchard to the simulator

Do pomar real ao simulador

Almost two years inside one simulator.

Quase dois anos dentro de um simulador.

ContextContexto

A training simulator for the operation of an apple orchard under hail net: spraying inside the rows, bins, the packing shed, forklift and yard, all in one environment. Team project at RealDrive for Agropecuária Schio, in Vacaria (RS), which presents itself as Brazil's largest apple producer.

Um simulador de treinamento para a operação de um pomar de maçã sob tela antigranizo: pulverização dentro do corredor, bins, barracão, empilhadeira e pátio, no mesmo ambiente. Projeto em equipe na RealDrive para a Agropecuária Schio, em Vacaria (RS), que se apresenta como a maior produtora de maçã do Brasil.

Before modelling, I went thereAntes de modelar, eu fui até lá

In August 2024 I joined the technical visit to the orchard. Hail net, row width, posts, bare winter branches, the road between blocks, bins, shed and silo: each detail became a reference. A hail-net orchard has its own rules: the net organises the rows and changes the light, the bin is what the operation moves, and the shed has to fit a manoeuvring forklift.

Em agosto de 2024 participei da visita técnica ao pomar. Tela antigranizo, largura do corredor, postes, galhos sem folhas no inverno, a estrada entre as quadras, bins, barracão e silo: cada detalhe virou referência. Um pomar com tela tem regras próprias: a tela organiza as linhas e muda a luz, o bin é o objeto que a operação movimenta, e o barracão precisa caber uma empilhadeira manobrando.

Trainings and rulesTreinamentos e regras

On top of the existing lesson architecture, I implemented and integrated the tasks of the trainings:

  • Industrial silo operation, bin transport and stacking, truck loading, monocalibre (fruit sizing machine) operation, orchard sprayer, shredder, trailer driving and parking, and forklift operation
  • Lesson progression: tasks, checkpoints, objectives passed and failed, final score and completion percentage
  • Penalties for collisions with people, vehicles, trees and structures; bin drops and apple loss; rollover; wrong use of the PTO, clutch and 4×4; RPM above the operating limit; implement damage
  • Safe lesson restart that keeps the student, instructor and selected training

Sobre a arquitetura de lições existente, implementei e integrei as tarefas dos treinamentos:

  • Operação do silo industrial, transporte e empilhamento de bins, carregamento de caminhões, operação do monocalibre (calibrador de frutas), atomizador, triturador, condução e estacionamento de carreta, e operação de empilhadeira
  • Progressão da aula: tarefas, checkpoints, objetivos concluídos e reprovados, pontuação final e percentual de conclusão
  • Penalidades por colisão com pessoas, veículos, árvores e estruturas; queda de bins e perda de maçãs; capotamento; uso incorreto da tomada de força (PTO), da embreagem e da tração 4×4; RPM acima do limite operacional; dano ao implemento
  • Reinício seguro da aula, mantendo aluno, instrutor e treinamento selecionado

1 · Grab without collisions1 · Pegada sem colisão
Problem

The hand colliders pushed the object while it was being held, so it shook or slid out of line in the hand.

Solution

When the object is grabbed, it becomes kinematic and ignores collisions with every collider of that hand; on release, gravity and collisions come back.

Result

A stable grip, without changing the hand rig or the physics of the rest of the scene.

void OnGrab(SelectEnterEventArgs args) {
    rb.isKinematic = true; rb.useGravity = false;
    handColliders = args.interactorObject.transform
                        .GetComponentsInChildren<Collider>();
    foreach (var c in handColliders)
        Physics.IgnoreCollision(myCollider, c, true);
}
// OnRelease: undo both

2 · Two-handed scaling2 · Escala com as duas mãos
Problem

Resizing an object in VR with a slider or a menu breaks the feeling of holding it.

Solution

Track the two hands holding the same object. When the second hand grabs, store the distance between them and the current scale; then the scale follows the change in distance.

Result

Scaling with a natural gesture: pull the hands apart to make it bigger, bring them together to make it smaller.

if (interactors.Count == 2) {
    float d = Vector3.Distance(interactors[0].transform.position,
                               interactors[1].transform.position);
    transform.localScale = initialScale * (d / initialDistance);
}

Mudar o tamanho de um objeto em VR por slider ou menu quebra a sensação de estar segurando.

Acompanhar as duas mãos que seguram o mesmo objeto. Quando a segunda mão pega, guardar a distância entre elas e a escala atual; depois, a escala segue a mudança dessa distância.

Problem

The menus were built with normal Unity UI buttons. Rewriting them for the headset's touch system would tie the whole interface to one XR SDK.

Solution

A small bridge: the touch (poke) event calls the normal button's onClick. A second script sinks the button a centimetre while it is pressed, so the finger feels it.

Result

The same menus work with touch, with the controllers and in the Editor, and the interface does not depend on the XR SDK.

public void InvokeUIOnClick() {          // called by the poke event
    if (targetUIButton != null) targetUIButton.onClick.Invoke();
}
public void OnSelectEnter() {            // visual: sink while pressed
    var p = baseLocalPos; p.z -= pressedDepth; depth.localPosition = p;
}

4 · A keyboard in VR4 · Um teclado em VR
Problem

Registration forms need typing, and a headset has no keyboard. A keyboard that opens in a fixed place can end up behind the user or inside a wall.

Solution

Adapted the open-source MRTK keyboard (Microsoft): when a text field is selected, the keyboard opens in front of the user, at a set distance and height, using only the horizontal direction of the head.

Result

The keyboard always appears where the user is looking, level, and the caret shows only while typing.

Vector3 dir = head.forward; dir.y = 0; dir.Normalize();
Vector3 pos = head.position + dir * distance + Vector3.up * verticalOffset;
NonNativeKeyboard.Instance.RepositionKeyboard(pos);

1 · How the hook carries the load1 · Como o gancho carrega a carga
Problem

The load was held by a rigid joint. Standing still it was fine; with the crane moving fast, the load shook.

Solution

A kinematic pivot that follows the hook, and the load connected to it by a ConfigurableJoint: position locked, rotation limited on two axes, with a soft spring.

Result

The load swings and settles like a hanging load, and it can still be switched back to the rigid joint for comparison.

connectPoint = new GameObject("Connect Point").AddComponent<Rigidbody>();
connectPoint.isKinematic = true;                 // follows the hook
connectPoint.transform.SetParent(hook.transform, false);

joint = cargo.AddComponent<ConfigurableJoint>();
joint.connectedBody = connectPoint;
joint.xMotion = joint.yMotion = joint.zMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Locked;
joint.angularXMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Limited;   // swing
joint.angularZMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Limited;
joint.angularYMotion = ConfigurableJointMotion.Locked;
var spring = new SoftJointLimitSpring { spring = 500 };

2 · Lessons by steps2 · Aulas por etapas
Problem

A lesson needs to know when the box was picked, when it reached the right place and when it was released there.

Solution

A lesson manager with a list of steps. Each step has a pick marker, a target zone (a trigger) and two instructions. The hook tells the manager when a box is attached or released; the zone tells it when the box enters or leaves.

Result

The same code runs a one-box lesson and a several-box lesson: only the list of steps changes, in the Inspector.

public class CrateLessonStep {
    public GameObject crateIndicator;     // where to pick
    public CraneTriggerZone targetZone;   // where to leave it
    public string pickInstruction, deliverInstruction;
}
// hook  -> NotifyCratePicked / NotifyCrateDetached
// zone  -> OnTargetZoneEntered / OnTargetZoneExited

Problem

The package controlled the crane only from the keyboard. In VR there is no keyboard: the trainee presses buttons on a control in the hand.

Solution

A button API inside the crane controller: each direction has Press, Release and SetPressed, and the crane keeps moving while the button stays pressed. Hand poses for holding the pendant control.

Result

The crane is driven from VR buttons (hands or controllers), and the keyboard stays only as an Editor debug path.

public void Btn_HookUp_Press()   { btnHookUp = true; }
public void Btn_HookUp_Release() { btnHookUp = false; }
// ... same for down, left, right, forward, backward

if (buttonsEnabled) {
    if (btnHookUp)  MoveHookUp();
    if (btnForward) MoveCraneForward();
}
